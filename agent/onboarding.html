XTENSION CONTROL PLANE

Your extensions should justify their place in your browser.

Xtension inventories what is installed, explains capability exposure, tracks access changes, and helps you run reversible cleanup experiments. It does not invent usage telemetry.

01

Why “manage extensions”?

Xtension needs Chrome's management permission to see installed extensions, including disabled ones, read their declared permissions, and perform actions you explicitly request such as enable, disable, or uninstall.

Xtension never silently disables or removes another extension.
02

Local by default

Your inventory, verdicts, notes, disable-trial outcomes, alerts, recovery records, and locally observed version history live in Chrome's local extension storage.

Raw browsing history is not persisted by Xtension.
03

Workflow relevance is optional

Base mode does not require browsing history. If you later enable workflow relevance, Chrome asks for history permission and Xtension compares recent URLs locally against declared extension site scope.

Site overlap is context evidence, not proof an extension executed.
04

Cleanup stays reversible

Use 7-day or 30-day Trial Disable instead of guessing. Re-enabling during a trial becomes dependency evidence. Surviving a trial becomes cleanup evidence whose confidence depends on duration.

Trial completion never triggers automatic uninstall.
THE RULE

Capabilities, relevance, and intent are different things.

A powerful extension can be essential. A harmless extension can still be useless clutter. Xtension keeps those dimensions separate instead of compressing your browser into a theatrical 83/100 score.
